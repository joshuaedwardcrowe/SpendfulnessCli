## Premise

The context a reader needs before the problem makes sense. What exists
today, and what forces are at play. Link the GitHub issue.

## Problem

The specific challenge this decision resolves. Concrete, not abstract.

## Solution

What we're doing. One paragraph, no hedging, followed by whatever detail
the decision actually needs — real type and method names, verified
against source rather than described from memory.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it lost. This is the part that saves
future-you from relitigating it.

## Consequences

What gets easier, what gets harder, and what debt this knowingly takes
on.
